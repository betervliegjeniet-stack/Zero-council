## M1 — Engine core
- Pure TypeScript engine in `src/engine`: `createGame(options)` and `applyIntent(state, intent) → { state, events }`.
- Seeded RNG (mulberry32) stored in the state; same seed and intents give the same game.
- Setup: seats, faction starting pools, four stage piles with the expansion split over Stage II/III,
  Mandate and Finale decks. Stage-by-turn drawing with fall-through when a pile is empty.
- Turn loop: upkeep (Blight drain) → Crisis reveal → Whisper timer → dials → reveal → resolution → end of turn.
- Actions: Gather, Salvage, Sabotage, Contribute, Self-Quarantine, Repair, Medical Tax, Idle; costs,
  Embargo, fizzles, Conscription. Cost-related faction passives are in (Archivists, Iron Remnant,
  Scrubber Guild, Blight Wardens).
- Generic card layer: `reveal` and `unmitigated` EH changes, Influence pools, `mod:*` tags,
  `endsGameOnFail`. 15 of the 47 Crisis cards are fully covered by it; the others reveal and are
  logged as `CardSkipped` until their handlers arrive in M2. Mandate and Finale cards are drawn and
  revealed but not resolved yet.
- Total Collapse penalty, the Crucible with its tie-breaks, end conditions and outcomes.
- `npm run sim` runs random-action games headlessly and checks state invariants.
- 76 tests, including 550 random games across 4, 5 and 6 players.

Not in M1 (by plan): card handlers, Urn votes, active abilities, deals (M2); AI Councilors (M3).

## M2 — Cards, votes, abilities, deals
- A handler for every one of the 71 cards, each following its `digitalRuling` (`src/engine/cards/`).
  A startup assertion fails if a card id has no handler or a vote tag has no vote.
- The Black Urn (`votes.ts`): target, two-target, Aye/Nay, Mercy, Judas, unanimous and open votes; no
  self-votes; the Directorate's double ballot and tie-break (with "let it burn"); tie penalties.
- All six faction actives: Redact Name, Vent the Sector, Whispered Paranoia (plus Binding Oath),
  Martial Law, Inoculate and Subpoena. Martial Law is offered as a 5-second prompt before a personal
  Crisis penalty lands; the engine dry-runs the effect on a copy to see who would be hit.
- Deals (`deals.ts`): the seven term kinds, immediate terms on accept, pace limits, promise checking
  at the end of Phase 4, BETRAYED records, Binding Oath penalty, Self-Quarantine voiding.
- New turn mechanics: random dials, sequential reveal, queued actions, announced actions with liar
  exposure, LP pledges, volunteer prompts, Cannibalism targets, face-down Crisis, Overdrive, Reboot.
- A resumable task queue lets any card stop for votes, choices and bids, and continue afterwards.
- Tests: 190 in total. One or more per card id (with a check that no id is missed), the Urn rules,
  every ability, the deal rules, and 550 random games with zero errors or invariant violations.
- `design-pack/` now holds the "Zero Hour" art bible PDF and its four card plates (for M4 art).

## M3 — AI Councilors
- `src/engine/ai/`: personalities (faction default ± 0.15 seeded jitter), difficulty temperatures, and
  beliefs (trust, hostility, LP estimate per rival) built only from public events. AIs never read hidden
  LP unless Merciless is played with `aiCheats`.
- Action choice by utility scoring and softmax: Gather, Salvage, Sabotage (target by threat, hostility
  and revenge), Contribute (harm if unmitigated, free-rider estimate, fair share, reserves), Quarantine.
  Promises are honored by honesty and trust; binding deals are nearly always kept.
- Voting per ballot type, the Directorate's tie-break, Redact Name, Martial Law, volunteer and card
  choices, LP pledges, Finale bids and Crucible bids.
- Whisper: Vent, Inoculate and Subpoena use; deal offers that fit the personality (shared Contribute,
  paid non-aggression, "Sabotage the leader with me", vote pacts, the Wardens' cure for sale); deals are
  accepted when their value after a trust discount is positive; the Choir binds deals it distrusts.
- The Bathroom Break Rule as an intent (`HumanAbsent`): the AIs' hostility toward the human rises.
- 270 flavor lines in `ai/lines.ts`: 5 per faction and message type.
- `npm run sim` now plays the AI Councilors and prints the full balance report; `--policy random`,
  `--difficulty`, `--brief` and `--ai '{...}'` (try weights without editing config) are available.
- AI weights were tuned (weights only, no rules): pool mitigation went from 19% to 59%, inside the
  40-70% target. Two factions miss the 8% survivor floor; see the balance findings in
  `docs/DESIGN_QUESTIONS.md`.
- Tests: 211 in total, including 500 AI games across 4, 5 and 6 players with no errors or violations.
