# Card art prompts

55 cards.
Prompts for the 55 cards that have no painted art yet. Each one combines the card's `artDirection` from `data/cards.json` with the style of the painted plates in `design-pack/plates/`.

To use a generated image: save it as `public/art/cards/<ID>.webp` (about 600 px wide, landscape or square crop of the illustration only) and run `node scripts/prepare-art.mjs`. The game then shows it instead of the vector art.

**Shared style line (append to every prompt):**

> Style: dark graphic-novel illustration with heavy black ink outlines and gritty painterly colour, dystopian bunker sci-fi with organic bio-horror, dramatic rim light, deep obsidian shadows fading to black at the bottom edge. Portrait card illustration, single clear focal subject, no text, no letters, no numbers, no borders, no UI gauges.
